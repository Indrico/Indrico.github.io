01 About

Most of my work happens where users never look.

The queue that absorbs a sudden spike. The webhook that quietly syncs a delivery status. The index that makes a heavy query fast again. That's where I do my best work.

For more than six years I've built and operated production systems for enterprise clients across banking, insurance, logistics and government. That includes a multi-tenant omnichannel platform that moves 100K+ transactions a day across WhatsApp, SMS and Email.

I like owning things end to end. I design the flow, write the code, ship it, and stay with it in production. That means tracking down database contention, draining worker backlogs, and fixing webhooks when they fail at the worst possible moment.

Employer and client names are withheld to respect confidentiality agreements.

An omnichannel messaging platform for enterprise tenants

Challenge

02 Architecture · Reliability

Queue architecture with a durable outbox

Challenge
Workloads have different urgency. A large broadcast shouldn't hold up a time-sensitive message, and no job may silently disappear.
What I did
Designed queue-based async processing with workload-specific workers, durable outbox processing, backlog monitoring and operational alerts through Sentry and Telegram.
Outcome
Isolated workloads, and backlogs or failures that arrive as alerts instead of surprises.
  • Queues
  • Outbox
  • Sentry
  • Telegram alerts
03 Data · Performance

Keeping a multi-million-row MySQL fast

Challenge
High-volume workloads on tables with millions of records led to heavy queries, database contention and worker backlogs.
What I did
Optimized queries and indexes for the hot paths, and investigated contention and application performance directly in production.
Outcome
Resolved production issues involving contention, heavy queries and worker backlogs, fixed at the root rather than patched over.
  • MySQL
  • Indexing
  • Profiling
04 Infrastructure · Migration

Rescuing a legacy Ubuntu 14 production server

What I did
Migrated the system into a KVM virtual machine and configured networking, IP assignment and automatic VM startup.

The legacy application kept running with minimal application changes, on infrastructure that can be managed and restarted predictably.

06 How I work

Four habits I bring to every system

01

Own it end to end

From the first commit to production troubleshooting, I build it, run it, and fix it when it breaks.

02

Design for failure

Providers go down and webhooks arrive late. Queues, outboxes and fallbacks turn that into a non-event.

03

Observable by default

If a backlog grows or a worker dies, I want to know first. Monitoring and alerts ship with the feature, not after the incident.

04

Protect the data

Clients trust us with their data. Encryption and careful access control are defaults, not add-ons.
